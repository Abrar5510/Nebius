Every clip starts as real footage of a real street. Then NVIDIA Cosmos imagines what could happen next. Click the moment you see a hazard developing. The earlier you click, the more you score.

How the clips are made
  1. Nemotron 3 Nano Omni reads the street: parked vehicles that hide people, side lanes, who uses this road.
  2. Nemotron 3 Ultra plans hazards that fit that street and the city's real crash causes, found live with Tavily.
  3. Cosmos Predict 2.5 renders each hazard as a continuation of the real footage, on a Nebius GPU job.
  4. Cosmos Reason 2 watches every render, writes the answer key and throws out anything physically implausible.

Click or press space when you see a hazard developing.

A hazard is anything that would make you slow down, steer or stop. You can't pause or rewind. Clicking constantly scores zero.
