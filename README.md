# Near Miss

**Hazard-perception training generated from your own streets.**

Record ten seconds of the road you drive every day. Near Miss uses NVIDIA Cosmos to imagine what could go wrong there next (a child running out from behind a parked rickshaw, a bike cutting across from a side lane), then tests whether you spot it in time.

Most countries with the deadliest roads have no hazard-perception test at all. The UK and Australia do, and in an NSW cohort of 20,822 novice drivers, those who failed the test twice crashed about 1.8x more often. Near Miss makes that kind of test for any street, in any city.
